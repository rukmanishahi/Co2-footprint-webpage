 🌍 CO₂ Daily Activity Tracker

A web app that estimates your daily carbon footprint just describe your day in plain English and it does the rest.

**Live site:** [rukmanishahi.github.io/Co2-footprint-webpage-withClaude](https://rukmanishahi.github.io/Co2-footprint-webpage-withClaude/)

## what it does

you type out what your day looked like what you ate, how you got around, what you had running at home and the app figures out your CO₂ emissions from all of it. it breaks everything down activity by activity, shows you a donut chart, gives you a colour-coded result (low / moderate / high), and then suggests ways you can actually cut down your impact based on your specific day.

no forms to fill out, no dropdowns, just write like you're texting a friend.

## features

- plain English input: describe your day however you want
- emissions calculated using IPCC/IEA factors for India
- activity-by-activity breakdown with a donut chart
- colour-coded verdict so you know where you stand at a glance
- personalised tips based on what you actually did that day
- local community action suggestions too
- your API key gets saved to the browser paste it once, done
- fully responsive on mobile
- magazine-style UI because why not make it look nice

## tech stack

- HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript
- Anthropic API
- Chart.js
- Google Fonts Playfair Display, DM Mono, Libre Baskerville
- hosted on GitHub Pages

## how to run it

1. grab an API key from [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com)
2. open the site and paste your key in the bar at the top
3. describe your day and hit **Analyse My Day**

no installs, no setup, runs entirely in the browser.

## why I made this

most carbon tracking tools make you fill out a hundred fields and half the time you don't even know what they're asking. I wanted to make something where you just describe your day like a normal person and it handles the rest. built this as a personal project to get hands-on with APIs and build something that's actually useful.
